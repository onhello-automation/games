Stretch

How to play
  • Guess the hidden word. You know only the range of lengths it could have.
  • Green letters are in the right spot.
  • Yellow letters are in the word, but in a different spot.
  • Each guess also tells you whether the word is longer, shorter, or the same length.

Examples

Say the hidden word is MAPLE.

  • place= same lengthE is in the right spot. P, L, and A are in the word, but in other spots. C is not in the word.
  • maples⬇ shorterThe first five letters are right, S is not in the word, and the word is shorter.
